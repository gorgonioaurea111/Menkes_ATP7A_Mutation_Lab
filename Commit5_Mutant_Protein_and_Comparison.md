## Interpretation

The sequence comparison shows that c.2179G>A produces a single amino-acid substitution, p.Gly727Arg (G727R). No downstream amino-acid sequence change was observed, and no premature stop codon was produced.

The computational analysis therefore supports the sequence-level relationship:

**c.2179G>A → GGA-to-AGA codon change → Gly727Arg substitution**

## Conclusion

The documented ATP7A mutation is a missense single-nucleotide substitution that changes Gly727 to Arg. The mutation does not cause a frameshift or alter the 1,500-amino-acid protein length. The major sequence-level difference between WT and mutant ATP7A is the single amino-acid substitution at position 727.
