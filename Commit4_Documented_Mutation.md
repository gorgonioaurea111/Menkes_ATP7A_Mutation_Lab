## Documented Variant

**NM_000052.7(ATP7A):c.2179G>A**

## Molecular Consequence

The c.2179G>A mutation produces a single amino-acid substitution at position 727, changing glycine (G) to arginine (R). Because only one nucleotide is substituted, the reading frame and overall protein length remain unchanged.

The sequence analysis supports the DNA-to-protein change:

**c.2179G>A → GGA-to-AGA codon change → p.Gly727Arg (G727R)**

The mutation may affect ATP7A structure and function. Published experimental evidence has reported reduced amounts of mutant ATP7A protein due to increased degradation and impaired copper-transport activity.
